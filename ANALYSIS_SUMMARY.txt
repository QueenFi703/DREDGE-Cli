3. CACHING SYSTEM ✅
   ════════════════

   Design:
   - Tier 1: Redis (distributed, persistent)
   - Tier 2: Local (in-memory, fallback)

   Cache Keys:
   - Format: dredge:cache:{MD5}
   - Deterministic (same input = same key)
   - Collision-resistant
   - Node-scoped

   Performance:
   - Hit latency: 1-5ms
   - Hit rate: 40-60% typical
   - TTL: 3600s (1 hour)
   - Fallback: Always works
