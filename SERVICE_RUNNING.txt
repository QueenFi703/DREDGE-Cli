🚀 FOR PRODUCTION
=================

Replace development server with:

Gunicorn:
  gunicorn --bind 0.0.0.0:3000 \
    --workers 4 \
    --timeout 60 \
    dredge.server:app

uWSGI:
  uwsgi --http :3000 --wsgi-file dredge/server.py \
    --callable app --processes 4

Nginx Reverse Proxy:
  Configure Nginx to forward requests to:
  http://127.0.0.1:3000
