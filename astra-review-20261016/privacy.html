Privacy policy

Effective October 16, 2026. Publisher and privacy contact: Sophia Cole / ColeWorld, fi@dredgeoriongateway.com.

Information processed

Authentication supplies a stable within-site account identifier and, where available, email. Owner workflows use that identity to enforce the pinned Siri owner and explicit plugin-memory consent. Active investigation workflows process submitted questions, research outputs, evidence, job identifiers, timestamps, usage and access records. Do not submit credentials, government identifiers, payment card details, protected health information or other sensitive case records.

Reviewer samples

Signed-in non-owner accounts receive prepared fictional data. Sample identifiers are derived for that account and another account cannot read them. This sample path does not query Siri investigations, persist new investigations or send model requests.

Use and recipients

Information is used to authenticate, authorize, deliver requested investigations, preserve opted-in context, enforce limits and troubleshoot. Hosting and authentication infrastructure includes OpenAI Sites and Cloudflare. Enabled AI and search workflows send necessary questions or context to the configured OpenAI services; approved public evidence sources may receive retrieval requests. If you select Arcade on the website, Arcade receives the question and an isolated session identifier to retrieve Google News evidence; the resulting evidence is passed to OpenAI for synthesis. Support email is processed by the email service used by the publisher. Provider retention and policies are separate from application retention.

Retention and access

New remembered investigations remain accessible for up to 90 days from creation. Transient Briefing jobs remain accessible for up to 24 hours. Follow-ups inherit the original parent expiry; existing records keep their original expiry. Expired records are denied at access time and removed during cleanup, which may occur later. The current owner history cap is 20 records. Operational metadata can remain longer than investigation access; no universal immediate-erasure guarantee is made.

Your controls

Remembered context requires explicit consent. The owner can revoke plugin-memory access at the account connection page; revocation stops plugin access but does not delete stored investigations or revoke paired Siri devices. Contact the privacy address for access, correction or deletion requests. Do not include private history or secrets in that request. Identity verification may be required. We do not sell personal data or use it for advertising through this plugin.
