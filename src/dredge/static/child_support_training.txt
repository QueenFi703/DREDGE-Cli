FICTIONAL TRAINING DATA — NOT AN AGENCY RECORD
Training case: Jordan Example
Record A: A fictional January notice lists a monthly amount of $900.
Record B: A fictional February record lists a monthly amount of $950.
Client explanation: The $950 amount began the following month; the earlier record describes the previous month.
Review question: Which period and source order does each record refer to?
No official balance is calculated. No fraud, liability or enforcement finding is made.
