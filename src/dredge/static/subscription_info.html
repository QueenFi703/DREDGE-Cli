Subscription access

General information about account entitlements and the read-only ChatGPT integration.

Account access

Paid access is recognized from a verified, active, unexpired live subscription associated with the signed-in DREDGE account. Sandbox subscription records are test records and do not grant paid access.

This page describes general access rules. It does not show any account's subscription status.

Workspace roles

Workspace roles and permissions are assigned separately. A subscription does not grant or change operator, reviewer, or administrator permissions. Authorized workspace actions remain subject to their existing role and approval requirements.

Stored reads

Existing owner-only stored reads remain available regardless of subscription status, with sign-in and the required authorization scopes. Optional run-reading tools must also be enabled and separately authorized.

The ChatGPT tools read account access, aggregate Studio activity, and authorized stored run information. They do not execute runs, modify records, or grant permissions.

AI usage

Paid AI usage is not included. These read-only tools do not call AI providers. Separate provider configuration, permissions, and any applicable usage charges remain outside these subscription entitlements.

Missing recorded usage or cost remains unknown and must not be represented as zero.
